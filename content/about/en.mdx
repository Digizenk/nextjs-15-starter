# About Digizenk

Digizenk makes AI prompt packs, printables and digital art for people who run small businesses, sell on Etsy or create content, and would rather spend their time on the work only they can do.

## What you'll find here

- **Guides** that walk through a real task step by step, with the prompts included so you can copy them straight into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini.
- **Prompt packs** built around one kind of business at a time, so the prompts fit your work without rewriting.
- **A free starter pack** of 25 prompts for the jobs every small business repeats each week.

## In English and Spanish

Everything on this site is written in both English and Spanish, by people, for people. Switch languages any time from the menu at the top of the page.

Have a question about a product or an idea for a guide? Reply to any Digizenk email and it comes straight to us.
