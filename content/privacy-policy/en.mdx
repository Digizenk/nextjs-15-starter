# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 1, 2026_

This policy explains what information Digizenk collects when you visit this website, and how it's used.

## What we collect

- **Your email address**, only if you sign up for the free prompt pack or our updates. We use it to send you the pack and occasional new guides.
- **Basic usage data**, such as which pages are visited and roughly where visitors come from. This is collected by analytics tools and doesn't identify you by name.
- **Advertising cookies**, if ads are shown on this site. Ad providers such as Google may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites.

## What we don't do

We don't sell your email address or share it with other businesses for their marketing.

## Purchases

Products are sold through Etsy and Hotmart. When you buy, your payment and order details are handled by those stores under their own privacy policies.

## Services we use

- **Resend** to store subscriber emails and send messages.
- **Google Analytics**, **Plausible** and **Vercel Analytics** to understand site usage.
- **Google AdSense** to show ads, where enabled.

## Your choices

- Unsubscribe from emails at any time with the link at the bottom of every message.
- Block or delete cookies in your browser settings.
- Ask us to delete your email address by replying to any Digizenk email.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
